Frequently Asked Questions

Booking & Reservations

You can easily book a ride through the booking form on our homepage or the "Book Now" page. Simply select your service, enter the required details like pickup/drop-off locations and dates, choose your vehicle, and complete the booking process.

Yes, absolutely. When making the booking, please enter the passenger's full name and contact number in the designated fields. All confirmations and communications will be sent to the email address you provide during booking.

We recommend booking at least 24 hours in advance to ensure vehicle availability, especially during peak seasons like Ramadan and Hajj. However, we do our best to accommodate last-minute bookings based on availability.

Payments & Pricing

Yes, the price you see at the time of booking is the final price you pay. It includes all taxes, tolls, and fees. There are no hidden charges.

We accept payments via Bank Transfer and Cash on Pickup. For bank transfers, you will be required to upload proof of payment to confirm your booking.
